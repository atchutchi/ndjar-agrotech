# N'djar Source Map

This document records the useful project sources reviewed before implementation.

## Primary Sources

The mobile prototype image defines the current MVP surface: 24 app screens covering splash, onboarding, login, registration, home, map, region detail, crops, crop detail, calendar, soil sample, livestock, doctors, consultation, chat, forum, library, subscription, contact and profile.

The final pitch decks define the problem: inappropriate land use, weak access to agricultural information, low digital inclusion and the need for pH and land aptitude information.

The executive summaries define the market and business model: farmers, cooperatives, NGOs, private sector, subscriptions, app, web, USSD and agricultural doctor services.

The participatory diagnosis reports define the first pilot context: southern Guinea-Bissau, especially Quinara, Buba, Sare Donha, Uane and Ugui.

For the MVP, Sare Donha 1 and 2, Uane and Ugui are the first safe pilot communities. The diagnosis gives field-observed crops and operational context, but not enough laboratory evidence to treat pH values as final.

The agricultural calendar spreadsheets define the first calendar content structure: seasons, rainy months, dry months, soil preparation, planting, weeding and harvest.

The USSD licence document defines future constraints for short-code operation.

## Data Confidence

The prototype pH values are useful for interface design but must not be treated as production data.

The reports support the pilot area and problem framing.

The crop and pH recommendations need agronomic review before production use.

The pH method must be stored because pH in water and pH in calcium chloride can differ.

## Repository Rule

Private legal, identity, banking and company registration documents must remain outside the public repository.
